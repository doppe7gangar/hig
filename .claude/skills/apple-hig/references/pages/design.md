# Design incredible apps and games that integrate seamlessly with Apple platforms.

- [Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) — Get the latest guidance and best practices for creating great experiences for any Apple platform.
- [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/) — Take advantage of official templates for Figma and Sketch, color guides, and more.
- [Icon Composer](https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/) — Build app icons for the new design, and apply real-time dynamic properties and other effects.
- [SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/) — Browse a library of over 7,000 symbols designed to integrate seamlessly with Apple platforms.
- [Pass Designer](https://developer.apple.com/pass-designer/) — Create passes for Apple Wallet on your Mac.
- [Reality Composer Pro](https://developer.apple.com/reality-composer-pro/) — Rapidly iterate, preview, and prepare 3D content.
